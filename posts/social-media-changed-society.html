Everything Wrong With Social Media

Social media is a failed invention. By failed, I mean social media just completely failed it's promise to keep everyone connected. Now instead it is a way to make humans more addictive and useless creatures.

  • Millions are wasting time online.
  • Worried about someone else's issue.
  • Distracted.
  • Following stupid trends.
  • Spending more and pretending to be rich.
  • Privacy is disappearing, and it is normalized as hell.
  • Comparing ourself with everyone better than us.
  • Values getting measured by likes and upvotes.
  • Emotions are no longer real.

Day by day it's getting more scarier like a sci-fi movie i watched years go. seariously, i can't find the world is moving to where with social media. Everyone is very busy enough to put their head inside their phone and nobody have anymore time to just talk or sit alone.

People know it's not worthy to spend their time online arguing with someone, criticizing someone else for their own decisions, caste and political related issues, etc. If this all just limited to a club or 2 or 3 persons, then it is completely ok. But daily millions of people consuming all this when actually they have to do nothing about it at all. what they can do? Because they watched some posts or worried about things all night, it is gonna stop? Never. It will exist as everyday it existed. nothing gonna change at all. But still these people can't stop scrolling or watching an infinite amount of contents all day long.

Nowadays instead of actually enjoying things, people seek perfection and progress tracking. While capturing moments or occassions for social media, even if it's not their job, they still forgot to actually enjoy the moment. All their focus is on a perfect post. Also once they created an account on any social media and start making progress by harvesting likes, losing that account is like a death threat. Many other's are just like skipping their meals, workouts and healthy habits for social media.

Seriously, am feeling enough and tired of living in such a world. It's not mental instability, or any trauma related issues at all. It's how the world changed recently as the result of social media influence. And sadly, only a small portion of people's actually understand and feel it. Those people's are living in a actual hell with a cursed life.
